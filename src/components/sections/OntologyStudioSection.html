01 · Ontology Studio

Let AI understand your business like a domain expert

An ontology is more than a knowledge graph. It lifts a business from prose people can read into a semantic asset machines can query, validate and execute: goals, objects, relations, rules, states, exceptions and evidence chains — plus actions that run under control.

Why an ontology

General models don't know a company's tacit industry knowledge or SOPs. We once tried to solve every complex business problem with prompts alone — the result was hallucination, unreliable execution and failed compliance. Only after unifying 55 separate WMS instances under one semantic architecture did agents truly understand what “SKU”, “dock” and “wave” mean.

From business evidence to executable knowledge: one lifecycle

  1. 01Define the goal

    Break the business goal into a tree of competency questions (CQs)

  2. 02Add context

    Real processes, roles, systems and open questions

  3. 03Design the top level

    Stable abstract classes, properties and relationships

  4. 04Build the business ontology

    Scan sources; generate nodes, relations, rules, exceptions and evidence

  5. 05Validate

    Test questions · Recall Profile · issue feedback loop

  6. 06Deliver

    Skill + MCP · A2A · scoped tokens handed to agents

AI-guided conversational build

Describe the business goal in natural language in Chat while the Ontology Base sidebar tracks creation stage, task status and progress; streaming output, multi-turn history, source scanning, proposals and a build workspace.

Graph workbench & Cloud Graph

Graph and list views, three semantic levels — Business Domains / Galaxies / Nodes — node properties and direct connections, minimap and search: from the global picture down to local business clusters.

Data binding & actions

Configure data sources and object-relation bindings; action definitions, context snapshots, write-permission re-checks, idempotent event outbox, timeout reclamation — executed by the independent Ontology Runtime.

Quality feedback loop

Retrieval tests, question banks and coverage, Recall Profiles, issue tracking, event rollback, version backup and restore.

Openness & governance

IAM/OAuth, platform RBAC, ontology and workspace permissions, open tokens, MCP and A2A, audit and token-usage views, multi-tenancy.

Local modelling & backup

ontology-builder-native lets consultants model in their own AI tools and sync to Studio; ontology-job provides scheduled logical backups and command-line restore.
